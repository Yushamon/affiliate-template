# Comparison Product Visibility Policy

The comparison data set may be broader than the visible editorial decision flow.

1. Finalists receive the deep decision experience.
2. A visible alternative needs an independent, data-supported use case.
3. Products without that use case remain available in the explorer, technical data layer, server-rendered depth, or internal links.
4. The editorial flow is curated; it is not a complete catalog or a fixed Top-N list.
5. Score is a quality indicator, not an automatic purchase recommendation. Fit and documented trade-offs determine the recommendation.
6. Future comparisons may have one finalist, two finalists, three close candidates, or no universal winner.
