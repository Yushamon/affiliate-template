# Information Architecture

## Rendered architecture

The indexable graph contains 255 nodes and 5,698 unique meaningful links from header/main content (6,897 when footer and all document regions are included). The homepage exposes 19 first-level destinations. Every reachable indexable content page is within three clicks.

| Class | Count | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Adequately linked | 246 | No action |
| Weakly linked | 4 | Editorial opportunity, not breakage |
| True orphan | 1 | Route/intent decision required |
| Intentionally isolated utility | 4 | Keep calm and low-prominence |

CategoryExperience hubs are frozen controls and all six are depth 1. The two other hub-like pages are aligned with the current structure: `/smarte-haustiertechnik/` is depth 1 and `/futterautomat-und-ernaehrung/` is depth 2. Neither is a legacy hub experience.

## Ownership observations

- `/futterautomat-berater/` and `/berater/futterautomat/` both serve feeder-advisor intent but provide different functional journeys. The former is orphaned; the latter is linked through the existing Advisor CTA and `/kaufberatung/`. Resolve route ownership before adding a link or redirect.
- Nassfutterautomat demand is fragmented across a category guide, nutrition guides, and a comparison. Current signals are too small to prove cannibalization or justify a new page.
- Manufacturer, Product, Comparison, Guide and Category systems remain structurally distinct and were not redesigned.

No indiscriminate internal-link insertion is recommended. Use contextual links only after intent ownership is clear.
